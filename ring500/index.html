How to rate (one example)

A loop is a closed walk: follow the lines from node to node and come back to where you started. The loop is written without repeating the start at the end: “0 – 1 – 2 – 3” means 0→1→2→3→back to 0.

A. 0 – 1 – 2 – 3 ✓ traceable: every step follows a drawn line, and 3 connects back to 0.

B. 0 – 1 – 3 ✕ not a loop here: the dashed jump 1→3 has no line in the drawing.

Ring drawings work exactly the same way

A. 5 – 0 – 1 – 2 ✓ traceable: 5→0→1→2, and 2 connects back to 5 along the drawn line.

B. 0 – 1 – 3 ✕ not a loop here: no line connects 1 to 3.

A branch that stops midway is not a loop either

A. 0 – 1 – 2 – 3 ✓ traceable: closes back to 0 along the square.

B. 0 – 1 – 4 – 5 ✕ not a loop: every step follows a line, but 5 is a dead end — the walk stops midway and never returns to 0. A branch off the loop does not close it.
